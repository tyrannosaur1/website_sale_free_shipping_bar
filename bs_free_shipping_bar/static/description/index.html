Free Delivery Progress Bar
A shopper who is twenty short of free delivery is a shopper who will find one more thing to buy, if the shop says so at the moment they are looking at the cart. This module says it, and stops saying it the moment the cart is above the threshold.
website_sale + deliveryCart and checkoutOPL-1
Goods only, always
The bar measures what the customer is buying. The delivery line is what the carrier charges, so it never counts towards free delivery, and a cart holding nothing else claims no progress at all.
Written in the cart's currency
The threshold is entered in the currency the site sells in. If a pricelist prices the cart in another one, the amount is converted, so the sentence is never about money the shopper is not spending.
No stale bar
Changing a quantity in the cart redraws the summary in place. The bar is part of that redraw, so it never keeps the amount from before the change.
What the customer sees
✓Below the threshold, a sentence with the amount still missing and a bar filled in proportion
✓At or above it, one sentence and a full bar, with no amount mentioned at all
✓The bar sits above the order total, in the cart and on every checkout step that shows the summary
✓Nothing on an empty cart, nothing while the feature is off, nothing when the threshold is zero
How it is configured
Three fields on the website, under Website › Configuration › Settings › Free Delivery:
Free Delivery BarThe switch. Off by default: a shop that has not said anything about free delivery must not start promising one.
Free Delivery ThresholdThe amount, in the website currency, at which delivery becomes free. Zero turns the bar off, exactly like the switch.
Amount ComparedWhether the goods total is read tax excluded or tax included. Delivery is excluded either way.
The edges, stated plainly
A cart with only delivery in itNo bar. Nothing has been bought, so nothing is on its way to free delivery, and a cart holding a delivery charge is not a purchase.
Exactly at the thresholdFree delivery, full bar. The comparison is made on the currency's rounding grid, so 99.9999 of a two-decimal currency is at the threshold, not a hair below it.
One cent shortThe sentence asks for that one cent, not for the whole threshold. The amount shown is the difference, rounded like the currency it is printed in.
Core's own delivery messageLeft alone. The delivery method's "free shipping from" note still appears where it does; this module adds the running total, it does not replace the carrier's terms.
Confirmed ordersNo bar: it speaks to a cart, not to an order that has already been placed.
